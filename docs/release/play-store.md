## English (en-IN, default)

**Title** (16): Parivar Panchang

**Short description** (76):
Gujarati panchang with tithi reminders for family birthdays, varsi and more.

**Full description:**

Parivar Panchang is a calm, private Hindu panchang made for Gujarati families, in Gujarati and English.

Remember your family's dates by tithi
Add a birthday by tithi, a varsi or shraddh, or an anniversary once, as a lunar date such as Kartak Vad 5 or from the English date it happened. The app works out the right day every year and reminds you the day before and on the day. Varsi can also remind you during Pitru Paksha. Your family's conventions are respected: printed-calendar day or afternoon timing for shraddh, two-day and skipped tithis, and extra (adhik) months.

Today at a glance
• Tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana and weekday, with the exact times they change
• Choghadiya, hour by hour, with "Next good time"
• Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika and Bhadra, clearly marked
• Sunrise, sunset, moonrise and moonset for your city

A month view shows the tithi on every day, with two-day and skipped tithis marked.

For families abroad
Living in New Jersey or London but following Ahmedabad's date? Choose the city whose sunrise decides your family's dates. Reminders arrive in time, wherever you are.

Private by design
No account. No ads. No tracking. No data collected. Everything is worked out on your phone and works without internet. Save a backup file whenever you like, and add every family date to your phone's calendar.

Made to be easy to read
Large, clear text that grows with your phone's font size, Gujarati or English throughout, and light and dark themes.

How it is calculated is explained in plain words in the app, along with how closely it matches published panchangs (sunrise within a minute, tithi end times within about two minutes).

Why "No" is accurate: Play counts data as *collected* only when it is transmitted off the device. Settings, family dates and cached calculations stay in the app's storage; backups and `.ics` files are created only when the user taps to save or share them, to a destination the user picks; "Report a mismatch" opens the user's browser and sends nothing unless the user submits it on GitHub. ## USE_EXACT_ALARM declaration

Play Console → App content → **Exact alarm permission** → core functionality: **Calendar app that shows event notifications**. Explanation text:

> Parivar Panchang is a Hindu lunar calendar (panchang). Its core feature is reminding users of their own family events that follow the lunar calendar: birthdays by tithi, varsi/shraddh death anniversaries and wedding anniversaries. Each event's date is recalculated every year, and the app shows a notification at the time the user chooses on the day before and on the day itself. These are user-facing calendar event notifications, which must arrive at that time even when the phone is idle; inexact alarms can be deferred by hours in Doze, which would make a day-of reminder arrive too late. The app schedules at most one alarm per event per lead day (a rolling 12-month window, capped at 400) and uses no other background work.
